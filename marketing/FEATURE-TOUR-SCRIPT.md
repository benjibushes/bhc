*The grand-tour video. Talking-head, one voice, walks the entire machine end
to end — buyer side, rancher side, founders, brand partners, the mission.
Built in modular chapters: shoot each as its own standalone clip (they double
as explainer reels), then stitch all of them into one 12–15 min feature video.
Pin the full cut on every profile, /links, and the homepage.*

*Voice: lowercase, direct, no hype words (BHC.md). Numbers [LIVE] — re-pull

company · buyer names never used · refund line verbatim at every share CTA.*

---

## HOW TO SHOOT IT

- Same setup as your talking-head reels: phone vertical, chest-up, one spot,
  eyes on lens. Captions burned in.
- **Shoot chapter by chapter.** 12 short takes are easier than one 14-minute
  take, and each chapter is a usable standalone reel on its own.
- For the full-video cut: read them in order, top to bottom. Transitions are
  written in.
- Where a chapter says *(optional screen-hold)* you can drop a 3–5s phone
  screen recording over your voice — but the video works 100% as pure talking
  head. Don't let missing b-roll stop you.
- Total spoken runtime ≈ 13 minutes at natural pace.
