## THE FORMAT (set it up once, never change it)

**The setup (identical every time — this is why it's repeatable):**
- Phone vertical on a tripod, chest-up framing, same spot every time (freezer,
  kitchen, or truck — pick one, own it).
- Eyes on the lens. One take. If you flub, keep rolling and say it again — cut
  the flub in edit, don't restart the shoot.
- Captions burned in after (80%+ watch muted). That's the only edit.
- Same sign-off every single video: **"real beef from a ranch near you —
  buyhalfcow.com."**

**The structure (every script fits this — memorize it, then you don't need a script):**
1. **HOOK (0–3s):** one sentence. A number, a claim, or a question. No "hey guys."
2. **BODY (15–45s):** the one idea. Never two. Plain words.
3. **CTA (3–5s):** one action. Comment keyword OR link in bio OR /sell. Never two asks.

**The batch (how you make 8 in an afternoon):**
- Sit down once a week. Read 8 scripts off this doc back-to-back into the lens.
- Same shirt, same spot, same energy. Nobody notices; the algorithm doesn't care.
- 8 talking-head reels = one hour of shooting + captions. That's two weeks of posts.

**The rule that makes it repeatable:** you are not making *videos*. You are
saying *one true thing* into a camera, on repeat, forever. The MATH pillar
alone can be remade every month with a fresh number. You will never run out.

## HOW TO NEVER RUN DRY (the 5 repeatable buckets)

Every script below is one of five shapes. When you need a new one, pick a
bucket and swap the variable:

1. **THE MATH** — "here's what [$ amount / cut / price] actually gets you." Swap the number.
2. **THE SYSTEM** — "here's the part the grocery store leaves out." Swap the stat.
3. **THE BUILD** — "here's what i did this morning building this." Swap the day.
4. **THE ANSWER** — "here's the honest answer to [question people ask]." Swap the question.
5. **THE RANCHER** — "ranchers: here's what you actually get." Swap the feature.

Remake any of these monthly with a new variable. That's the whole engine.
