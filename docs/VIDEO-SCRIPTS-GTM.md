## LANE 1 — WHAT IT IS (the anthem · ~80s · pinned everywhere)

**Job:** watch once, understand the whole company. Pinned on every profile,
/links, the site. Ranchers see it before calls; buyers send it to a spouse.

**Hooks (test as separate posts; ranked by critic):**
1. "beef hit a record $9.64 a pound this summer — and the ranchers who raised it are going broke. same problem. i built the fix."
2. "there are almost two thousand families on a waitlist to buy beef straight from a ranch. let me show you the line they're standing in." *[LIVE — re-pull count]*
3. "buying half a cow used to mean a month of phone calls and a guy who knows a guy. it's a 90-second thing now. here's what i built."

| time | shot | VO |
|---|---|---|

## LANE 2 — HOW IT WORKS FOR CUSTOMERS (~62s · buyer explainer)

**Job:** the "send this to your spouse" video. Walks the real product.
Shares → /access; small tickets → /shop. NO per-pound-savings claims
(banned — often untrue); sell provenance + flat-price freezer economics.

**Hooks:**
1. "here's how my family buys beef now — and why i'll never buy grocery beef again."
2. "the ranch two towns over will fill your whole freezer at one flat price per pound — steaks and ground alike. here's how that works."
3. "a warehouse store will sell you half a cow with no ranch name attached. here's the version where you know exactly who raised it."

**Script (hook 1 version):**

| time | shot | VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6s | FULL chest freezer, packs thudding down for a beat, then Ben. | "here's how my family buys beef now — and why i'll never buy grocery beef again." |
| 6–15s | Screen rec: quiz taps. On-screen: "90 seconds" | "you take a 90-second quiz — how much beef you eat, your freezer space, your timeline. no account, no sales call." |
| 15–27s | Screen rec: match → real ranch page (photos, family, certs). | "we match you to a verified ranch near you. not a warehouse — a family. their land, their practices, their prices, before you spend a dollar." |
| 27–39s | Screen rec: deposit screen. On-screen: "fully refundable until your rancher accepts" | "you reserve your share — whole, half, or quarter — with a deposit that's fully refundable until your rancher accepts you. nobody's money gets taken hostage." |

**Caption CTA:** take the 90-second quiz → buyhalfcow.com/access · start small → buyhalfcow.com/shop · (ranch pickup exists too — see the farmers market on /shop)

3. "i've got almost two thousand families waiting to buy beef straight from a ranch. if that's you, watch this." *[LIVE — re-pull count]*
