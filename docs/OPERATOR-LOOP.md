# The Operator Loop — exactly when Ben enters, per rail

The machine's doctrine (speed-to-lead, 2026-08-07): deposit-first,
human at defined moments ONLY. This doc is the complete list of those
moments. If a situation isn't on this page, the machine owns it.

## Signal taxonomy — how the machine summons you

| Signal | Meaning | Your response time |
|---|---|---|
| **LOUD Telegram card** | Money is live or leaking right now | Same hour |
| **Cockpit dial row** (/admin/today) | Worth a human touch today | Same day, in list order |
| **Normal Telegram card** | FYI / one-tap decision | When convenient |
| **Monday learning report** | Read + approve/ignore proposals | Weekly, 10 min |
| Anything else | The machine's job | Never |

## Rail 1 — Share deposit (Connect / tier_v2): the main money path

| Stage | The machine does | Customer moves forward when | BEN ENTERS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signup → quiz | Capture, score, lane, welcome | They finish the quiz | — |
| Reveal | Deposit-capable match → deposit page is THE CTA | **They pay the deposit** (the entire design pushes here) | — |
| Balked (opened link, didn't pay) | 14-day/5-touch invite arc + decay | Any touch converts | **ENTRY 1 — the balk call.** Live balk ping (loud) → call/text within minutes when you can (21x qualification inside 5 min). Otherwise they appear on the cockpit dial list next morning. This is your highest-$/minute moment. |
| Deposit PAID | Settle, notify rancher, start accept-SLA | Rancher taps Accept | **ENTRY 2 — unaccepted deposit.** Accept-SLA re-pings the rancher; escalation card (loud) means: call the RANCHER. Paid buyer waiting = the one truly unacceptable stall. |
| Accepted → cuts/processing talk | First-touch SLA: 48h nudge → 96h escalation | Rancher calls the buyer | **ENTRY 3 — silent rancher.** 96h card → call the rancher, not the buyer. |
| Final balance | Durable invoice + 3-touch dunning | Buyer pays | **ENTRY 4 — dunning exhausted.** Escalation card after 3 touches → one personal text. |
| 7d stuck anywhere | Close-detector card: Won / Lost / Working | — | **ENTRY 5 — one tap.** Not a call; a decision. Answer the card. |
| Closed Won | Commission already captured (fee on deposit) | — | — |

## Rail 2 — Shop products (nationwide)

Fully machine: buy → settle → order in rancher's Shopify (or dashboard)
→ fulfill → tracking to buyer → ladder-up nudges.
**BEN ENTERS once:** rancher-not-shipping SLA card → text the rancher.
(Missed income here = supply problem, not sales problem.)

## Rail 3 — Broker (deposit = your commission)

Machine: campaign/link → deposit paid to BHC → instant handoff email
with buyer contact + order to the rancher + your Telegram card.
**BEN ENTERS twice:** (1) the handoff moment — one intro text to both
sides keeps the relationship warm (optional but converts); (2) if the
rancher goes silent on the remainder — first-touch card → call them.

## Rail 4 — Supply (ranchers) — the always-human rail

Recruiting closes, price confirmations, Connect onboarding pushes,
relationship repair: ALWAYS you. The machine preps (call sheets,
scripts, one-click asks) and chases (stuck-rancher queue on the
cockpit), but a rancher signs because Ben called. This is the rail
where your minutes buy the most long-term revenue: every payable
rancher ≈ +$162/mo AND unlocks their state's stranded cohort.

## The daily 15 minutes (once cockpit parity C1-C3 ships)

1. Open /admin/today.
2. **Replies waiting** band: read AI-staged drafts, one-tap send or
   edit. (~2 min)
3. **Dial list**, top to bottom — each row says who, why, and the next
   best action. After each call, tap the outcome (No answer / Talked,
   follow up N days / Drop) — that tap is what keeps tomorrow's list
   honest. (~10 min, usually 1-4 calls)
4. Glance at the scoreboard: complaints green? deposit funnel moving?
   (~1 min)
5. Done. Everything else that happens today is the machine's job, and
   it will interrupt you (loud card) if money needs a human.

## What NEVER needs you (so you stop checking)

Nurture sends, window arcs, sunset asks, digest, reply logging and
classification, suppression, settlements, payout math, tracking
emails, SLA timers, lane reclassification, backups, keep-alives,
complaint monitoring, campaign waves (once autopilot is live past
shadow), variant bookkeeping. If any of these breaks, the machine
files the alarm — you'll never discover it by checking.
