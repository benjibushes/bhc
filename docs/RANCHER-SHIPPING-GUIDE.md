# Shipping beef that arrives frozen — the rancher guide

*2026-07-08. Give this to every rancher who lists a frozen product. The
platform handles the order, the money, and the tracking — this doc handles
the box. Written to be sent as-is (it's also the source for the dashboard
help link).*

---

## The one rule

**Beef arrives frozen or it was free.** Every decision below serves that
rule. The buyer promise on the shop is "wrong or freezer-burned? we make
it right — no forms," and the fastest way to never pay on that promise is
to ship like this.

## Shelf-stable products (jerky, sticks, seasonings)

Easy mode — no cold chain:
- Padded mailer or small box, USPS Ground Advantage or First Class.
- Real cost: usually $4–6. If you set $5 shipping (or bake it into the
  price and check "shipping included"), you're covered.
- Ship any day of the week.

## Frozen products (boxes, bundles, ground)

**The box:**
- Insulated liner (foam or recycled-fiber) inside a sturdy cardboard box.
- Beef goes in FROZEN SOLID — never fresh-chilled. Your freezer does most
  of the work; the box just has to hold the cold.
- Dry ice on top of the meat (cold falls): ~5 lbs per day of transit for
  a mid-size box. 1-day transit ≈ 5 lbs, 2-day ≈ 10 lbs. Gel packs are a
  backup for 1-day only, never for 2-day summer shipments.
- Fill dead air with crumpled kraft paper — air is the enemy.
- Mark the box "Contains dry ice — UN1845" with the weight (carriers
  require it; labels are cheap on Amazon).

**The calendar (this prevents 90% of disasters):**
- **Ship Monday–Wednesday ONLY.** A Thursday 2-day label that slips one
  day sits in a warehouse all weekend. Nothing survives that.
- Watch the forecast at the DESTINATION. 100°F week in Phoenix? Hold the
  order two days and tell the buyer — they'd rather wait than mop.

**The service:**
- 2-day maximum transit, period. UPS Ground actually delivers 1–2 day
  within ~2 zones — check the ground map for your area; ground at 1–2
  days is dramatically cheaper than air.
- Buy labels through Pirate Ship (free account, commercial rates, ~50–70%
  off retail). No integration needed — copy the tracking number into your
  dashboard and the buyer gets it automatically.
- If the buyer is more than 2 ground days away: use 2-Day Air and PRICE
  IT IN, or don't offer shipping that far yet. An honest "local +
  neighboring states only" beats a $60 air bill you ate.

**Pricing your shipping field:**
- Set your per-order shipping at the WORST case you're willing to serve
  (box + liner + dry ice + label ≈ $25–45 for a 10–20 lb box at 1–2
  zones). You keep 100% of it — better to clear $5 extra than eat $15.
- Or fold it into the product price and check "shipping included" — the
  card then says so, and buyers convert better on all-in prices.

**When it goes wrong:**
- Buyer says it arrived soft/warm: it's thawed-but-cold ≠ spoiled —
  beef that's still fridge-cold is safe and refreezable; say so kindly.
  Actually warm = it's gone: tell us (reply to any order email or your
  dashboard), we make it right with the buyer, and we'll figure out the
  reship together. Photos help, forms don't exist.
- One melted box handled generously earns a customer for life. Argue
  and you lose the customer AND the review.

## What the platform already does for you

- Collects the order + shipping money into your Stripe account (you keep
  100% of the shipping line; card processing on us).
- Emails + pushes your phone the moment an order lands.
- Sends the buyer their receipt, and their tracking the moment you paste
  the number into the fulfillment tab.
- Chases nothing from you by mail — everything is the dashboard.

## The 60-second checklist (tape it to the freezer)

1. Order push arrives → confirm stock in dashboard.
2. Frozen solid? Liner + dry ice (5 lb/transit-day) + kraft fill.
3. Label via Pirate Ship — ground if ≤2 days, ship Mon–Wed only.
4. Dry-ice sticker on the box.
5. Paste tracking into the dashboard → buyer's notified automatically.
6. Destination heatwave or Thursday? Hold till Monday, message the buyer.
