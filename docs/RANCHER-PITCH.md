# The Rancher Pitch — what BHC is, how to say it, why nobody else can

*2026-07-08. The canonical answer to "how do I describe my business model to a
new rancher." Use on calls, DMs, /sell copy, and the outreach engine. Voice
rules from docs/BHC.md apply — lowercase, direct, no hype words.*

---

## 1. The one-breath version

> "every beef tool out there sells ranchers software and leaves them to find
> their own buyers. i built the opposite — a demand engine. families come to
> buyhalfcow looking for a ranch, we qualify them, take their deposit, and
> route them to you until your capacity is sold out. you raise the beef and
> hand it over. that's the whole deal."

If they only remember one sentence: **"they sell you a cash register. we
show up with the line of customers."**

## 2. The model, rancher language (60 seconds)

- Families find us (40k+ following, the map, the shop, search). They take a
  qualification quiz — budget, freezer, timeline — so nobody unserious ever
  reaches you.
- A qualified buyer in your state gets routed to YOU. They put a real
  deposit down through your page. Not a lead — a paying customer.
- You raise, cut, and hand over the beef. The money lands in **your own
  Stripe account** — your brand, your customer list, your prices. We never
  own your customer.
- You get leads **until your capacity is actually sold out** — dead intros
  auto-expire and free the slot; lead flow only pauses on real live deals.
- Non-exclusive. Pause or leave whenever. Free to start.

Money (tier_v2 truth — never quote flat 10%):
- **Free tiers**: commission comes out of the buyer's deposit (Legacy
  Connect 10% · Pasture 7% · Ranch 3%). You pay nothing until money moves.
- **Operator — $500/mo, 0% commission**: "we close, you ship." Ben runs the
  sales calls, the follow-up, the deposit chase. You get texts that beef is
  sold.

## 3. Everything they get (the feature wall — say 5, have 30)

**A storefront, done for them**
- Public ranch page (photos, story, certs, FAQ, delivery, pricing) — live
  the day they finish a 5-minute wizard
- Listed on the discover map + the nationwide /shop marketplace
- Products they add ship-nationwide OR sell as local pickup in the shop's
  farmers-market rail for buyers in their state — their choice per product,
  and the form tells them exactly where each product will appear
- They keep 100% of the shipping charge they set

**A sales machine, running for them**
- Quiz-qualified buyers only (budget + freezer + timeline screened)
- Routed by state + food-miles proximity — local families first
- Deposits collected on-platform into their own Stripe Connect account
- Automatic follow-up: stale intros expire, buyers get recovered, nobody
  falls through a crack a human forgot about
- Ben closes for Operator-tier ranches

**A back office they don't have to learn**
- Phone-first dashboard that installs like an app — push notification when
  a buyer lands, a deposit hits, an order pays
- One-input pricing, earnings tab, CSV export, fulfillment tracker,
  buyer messaging, capacity self-serve ("how many more can I take")
- No tech skills needed. If they can text, they can run it.

**The terms that answer every scar Crowd Cow left**
- Your brand and your customer — we route buyers TO you, never resell
- Your Stripe account — money never sits with us
- No consignment, no inventory risk, nothing "returned after 90 days"
- No setup fee, no monthly fee unless you choose Operator
- Cancel or pause routing any time

## 4. Competitive positioning (facts, sourced, ethical to say out loud)

| Them | What they cost | The truth | The line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barn2Door | $99–299/mo + $399–599 setup + up to ~5.9%/txn | Their own FAQ: they "teach farmers how to fish" — marketing is a paid add-on, demand is your problem | "they charge you $500 up front to teach you to find your own customers. we show up with the customers." |
| GrazeCart | ~$89–199/mo + 2.9% (double fee on catch-weight) | Reviews verbatim: "builds your store but doesn't help you get people to it" | "a nice cash register. it doesn't put a single person in line at it." |

| Crowd Cow | 22% + you eat processing, freight, packaging | Consignment; owns the customer; unsold product shipped back after 90 days at your expense (The Counter investigation, named ranchers burned) | "your brand, your price, your Stripe account — the exact opposite of that deal." |
| Doing it yourself | "free" | 60–70 hr weeks self-reported; 4 buyers per animal found, qualified, deposited, scheduled by hand; DMs + no-shows + chasing checks | "free tools, most expensive channel — you're paying in weekends." |

**The open lane (why this positioning holds):** nobody in the rancher-tools
market sells demand. The only demand aggregators are the wrong shape (jerky
boxes or anonymous directory clicks). "Qualified local buyer, deposit down,
routed into your own Stripe account" has no occupant. That's the moat until
someone copies it — which is why supply speed matters now.

## 5. Objections (their fears, verbatim answers)

- "Marketplaces steal your customers." → "the buyer pays into YOUR stripe
  account and lands on YOUR page. i physically can't own your customer —
  it's not how it's built."
- "I've paid for leads that never close." → "you don't pay for leads. free
  tiers cost you a cut of a deposit that already happened. no deposit, no
  cost, ever."
- "I don't do tech." → "if you can text, you're overqualified. five-minute
  setup, and the operator tier means i even do the selling."
- "What's the catch on 'free'?" → "the catch is i only make money when you
  do. that's the whole design."
- "How do I know the buyers are real?" → "every one passed a quiz — budget,
  freezer, timeline — and put money down before you spend a minute on them."
