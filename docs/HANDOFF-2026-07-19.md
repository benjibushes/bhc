## 5. CORRECTIONS (things I got wrong — do not repeat)

I was wrong **five times**, always the same way: **guessing an Airtable field
name instead of reading the schema.**

1. "Close is broken" — queried `Deposit Paid At` (new-model field) against
   legacy invoice deals. **Wrong.** Real fields: `Sale Amount`, `Commission Due`,
   `Commission Paid`.
2. "Cross-state blocked the deposit" — Champion Valley serves NE/**CO**/KS with
   `Admin Approved Multi-State`. **Wrong.**
3. "No deposit links exist" — queried `Deposit Link`; the real field is
   **`Deposit Checkout URL`**, and 6 of 7 have valid ones. **Wrong.**
4. "Invites never sent" — `Deposit Invite Sent At` was simply never *written*.
   Email Sends log shows `buyer_deposit_invoice` **sent**. **Wrong.**
5. "Buyers aren't receiving emails" — Resend says **delivered**. The open field
   was a dead gauge. **Wrong.**

**Rule for any future session: read the table schema before asserting anything
about a field. A blank field means "nothing writes here" at least as often as
it means "this didn't happen."**

`docs/MONEY-TRUTH-INVARIANTS.md` Part 1 is built on #3/#4 and is **wrong** —
links ARE minted and ARE sent. Its invariant idea is still good; rewrite it
around dead gauges + money-email capping.
